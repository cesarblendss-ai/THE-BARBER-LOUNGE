# On-Page SEO Audit — Yes We Can Mobile Solutions

## On-Page SEO Audit Checklist for Yes We Can Mobile Solutions

### CRITICAL (fix within 48 hours)
1. **Meta Title and Description**
   - **What to check:** Ensure the homepage and service pages have unique and optimized meta titles and descriptions.
   - **What good looks like:** Meta Title: "Mobile Beverage Canning in Antioch, CA | Yes We Can Mobile Solutions" (60 characters); Meta Description: "Expert mobile beverage canning services in Antioch, CA. We bring the canning line to you! Get in touch today!" (150 characters).
   - **How to fix it:** Use a plugin like Yoast SEO to edit title and description for each page.

2. **Homepage H1 Tag**
   - **What to check:** Ensure the H1 tag on the homepage includes primary keywords.
   - **What good looks like:** The H1 should state clearly: "Mobile Beverage Canning Services in Antioch, CA."
   - **How to fix it:** Edit the homepage HTML or through your CMS to update the H1 accordingly.

3. **Broken Links**
   - **What to check:** Use a tool like Screaming Frog to identify broken internal and external links.
   - **What good looks like:** No broken links; all links should lead to active pages.
   - **How to fix it:** Update or remove broken links from your content.

4. **Mobile Usability**
   - **What to check:** Run a mobile usability test through Google Search Console.
   - **What good looks like:** No mobile usability errors reported.
   - **How to fix it:** Optimize any issues indicated by Google (e.g., text too small, content wider than screen).

### HIGH PRIORITY (fix this week)
5. **Image Optimization**
   - **What to check:** Ensure all images have descriptive file names and alt tags including relevant keywords.
   - **What good looks like:** An image file named "mobile-beverage-canning-antioch.jpg" with alt text "Mobile Beverage Canning Service in Antioch, CA."
   - **How to fix it:** Renamed images and add alt text via the CMS.

6. **Internal Linking Structure**
   - **What to check:** Ensure key service pages link to each other and the homepage.
   - **What good looks like:** Each service page links to related services and has a clear path back to the homepage.
   - **How to fix it:** Add relevant internal links where necessary.

7. **Schema Markup**
   - **What to check:** Use Google’s Structured Data Testing Tool to see if schema markup is implemented.
   - **What good looks like:** Business information outlined with schema (LocalBusiness schema).
   - **How to fix it:** Add schema markup via a plugin or directly into the HTML.

### MEDIUM PRIORITY (fix this month)
8. **Content Updates**
   - **What to check:** Review service pages for outdated information or missing local keywords.
   - **What good looks like:** High-quality content detailing services, benefits, and local relevance.
   - **How to fix it:** Update content with relevant local keywords and detailed descriptions.

9. **Blog Section**
   - **What to check:** Create and publish relevant blog content to establish topical authority.
   - **What good looks like:** Two blog posts per month addressing local beverage trends, canning benefits, or recipes.
   - **How to fix it:** Use a content calendar to maintain a regular posting schedule.

### QUICK WINS (takes < 30 min each)
10. **Title Tag Format**
    - **What to check:** Ensure all title tags follow the format "Service - Location | Business Name."
    - **How to fix it:** Edit title tags through the website’s back end.

11. **Social Media Links**
    - **What to check:** Ensure social media icons are present and linked correctly.
    - **How to fix it:** Add or correct social media links in the site footer.
