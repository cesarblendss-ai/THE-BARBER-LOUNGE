## What Styles Can I Get for a Fade?

Choosing the right fade style can significantly impact your overall look. Here are several popular fade options offered at our barbershop in Antioch:

### Low Fade

The low fade is a subtle option that blends down to the neckline. It gives a clean look without being too dramatic, making it perfect for professional settings. 

### Mid Fade

This versatile style works well with various hair types and lengths. It starts midway down the sides of the head, blending into longer hair on top. The mid fade offers a nice balance between stylish and sophisticated.

### High Fade

For those who want to make a statement, the high fade offers a dramatic contrast between the hair on top and the faded sides. It pairs well with pompadours, quiffs, or other stylish hairstyles.

### Taper Fade

A taper fade gradually shortens the hair down to the neckline, providing a refined and clean appearance. This classic style pairs excellently with beards or other facial hair looks.

## How Can I Maintain My Fade?

Maintaining your fresh fade is crucial for keeping that sharp look. Here are some tips:

### Regular Appointments

To keep your fade looking fresh, schedule regular appointments every 2-4 weeks. This will help maintain the shape and style.

### Use Quality Products

Invest in good quality hair products. We recommend using lightweight pomades or waxes to help style your hair without weighing it down.

### Home Care

Maintain your hair by washing it with a sulfate-free shampoo, and use conditioner to keep it nourished and healthy. Avoid using too much product to prevent buildup.
