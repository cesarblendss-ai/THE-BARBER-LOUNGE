# On-Page SEO Audit — The Barber Lounge

### On-Page SEO Audit Checklist for The Barber Lounge in Antioch, CA

---

## CRITICAL (fix within 48 hours)

### 1. **Title Tag Optimization**
- **What to check:** Verify the title tag of the homepage and service pages.
- **What good looks like:** Titles should include "Barbershop in Antioch, CA" and relevant services (e.g., "Men's Haircuts," "Beard Trimming").
- **How to fix it:** Change the title tag to something like "The Barber Lounge | Expert Barbershop in Antioch, CA - Men's Haircuts & Grooming."

### 2. **Meta Description Accuracy**
- **What to check:** Check meta descriptions on the homepage and service pages.
- **What good looks like:** Descriptions should be engaging and include keywords relevant to local search intent.
- **How to fix it:** Create meta descriptions like, "Visit The Barber Lounge in Antioch for precision haircuts, beard grooming, and exceptional service."

### 3. **NAP (Name, Address, Phone Number) Consistency**
- **What to check:** Ensure NAP is accurate in the website footer, contact page, and across all platforms.
- **What good looks like:** Displays as “The Barber Lounge, 123 Sample St, Antioch, CA, (555) 123-4567”.
- **How to fix it:** Update the footer and contact page to reflect any discrepancies in the NAP.

### 4. **Schema Markup Implementation**
- **What to check:** Check if local business schema is implemented on the homepage.
- **What good looks like:** Valid schema code for a local business, including services and reviews.
- **How to fix it:** Implement JSON-LD schema using a local business generator to add to the header.

### 5. **Mobile Usability Issues**
- **What to check:** Use Google Mobile-Friendly Test tool.
- **What good looks like:** Pages are fast-loading and easily navigable on mobile devices.
- **How to fix it:** Optimize images and CSS, and check for excessive pop-ups affecting usability.

---

## HIGH PRIORITY (fix this week)

### 1. **Header Tags (H1, H2, H3) Structure**
- **What to check:** Ensure there’s a clear hierarchy of header tags on service pages.
- **What good looks like:** H1 for the main page title, H2 for each section (e.g., services, testimonials).
- **How to fix it:** Revise the header structure: H1 - "Barbershop Services in Antioch, CA," followed by specific services in H2.

### 2. **Content Optimization for Local Keywords**
- **What to check:** Review existing page content for local keywords such as “Antioch barbers,” “haircuts in Antioch.”
- **What good looks like:** Naturally integrated local keywords with a 1-2% keyword density.
- **How to fix it:** Add local context and keywords into service descriptions and blog posts.

### 3. **Image Alt Text Optimization**
- **What to check:** Review images on the homepage and service pages.
- **What good looks like:** Alt texts should describe the image and include relevant keywords.
- **How to fix it:** Add alt texts like “Men's haircut at The Barber Lounge in Antioch, CA”.

---

## MEDIUM PRIORITY (fix this month)

### 1. **Content Creation for Service Pages**
- **What to check:** Look for gaps in content related to key services (e.g., beard trimming, styling).
- **What good looks like:** Each service page should have 300-500 words of relevant, engaging content.
- **How to fix it:** Create detailed service descriptions answering common customer queries (e.g., "What is a fade cut?").

### 2. **Internal Linking Structure**
- **What to check:** Evaluate internal linking among service pages and blogs.
- **What good looks like:** Logical linking between services and related content to enhance user experience.
- **How to fix it:** Add links in service descriptions leading to related services or blog posts.

### 3. **User Reviews and Testimonials**
- **What to check:** Look at the user-generated content section for reviews.
- **What good looks like:** Display real reviews prominently on the homepage and service pages.
- **How to fix it:** Add a testimonials section and actively request feedback from customers.

---

## QUICK WINS (takes < 30 min each)

1. **Fix Broken Links**
   - **What to check:** Use Screaming Frog or a similar tool to find broken links.
   - **How to fix it:** Redirect broken links or update them to point to the correct URLs.

2. **Set up a Blog Section**
   - **What to check:** Create a blog section if not present.
   - **How to fix it:** Add a "Blog" link to the navigation menu.

3. **Add a Contact Page**
   - **What to check:** Ensure there’s a dedicated contact page.
   - **How to fix it:** Create a contact page with a form, phone number, and map.
