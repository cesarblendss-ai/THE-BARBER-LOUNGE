# On-Page SEO Audit Checklist for The Barber Lounge - Antioch, CA

## CRITICAL (Fix Within 48 Hours)

### 1. Check for 404 Errors or Broken Links
- **What to check:** Use tools like Screaming Frog or Google Search Console to identify broken links.
- **What good looks like:** No 404 errors; all internal and external links redirect correctly.
- **How to fix it:** Update or remove broken links. Create 301 redirects for necessary links.

### 2. Title Tag & Meta Description Optimization
- **What to check:** Assess existing title tags and meta descriptions for relevance to services and location.
- **What good looks like:** Title tags contain keywords (e.g., "Barbershop in Antioch, CA") and are 50-60 characters. Meta descriptions are 150-160 characters, enticing, and include a location.
- **How to fix it:** Rewrite title tags and meta descriptions to incorporate local keywords and encourage clicks.

### 3. Mobile-Friendliness
- **What to check:** Use Google’s Mobile-Friendly Test to assess mobile compatibility.
- **What good looks like:** 97% or higher score with no mobile usability errors.
- **How to fix it:** Optimize CSS and JavaScript. Ensure buttons and links are tappable on mobile devices.

### 4. SSL Certificate Check
- **What to check:** Ensure the site uses HTTPS.
- **What good looks like:** URLs start with "https://".
- **How to fix it:** Obtain and install an SSL certificate through your hosting provider.

## HIGH PRIORITY (Fix This Week)

### 5. Optimize Header Tags
- **What to check:** Review existing H1, H2, and H3 tags for keyword usage.
- **What good looks like:** One H1 tag that includes the primary keyword (e.g., "Antioch Barbershop"). Several H2 and H3 tags that include supporting keywords and local terms.
- **How to fix it:** Adjust headers to include local keywords relevant to services offered in Antioch.

### 6. Local Schema Markup
- **What to check:** Ensure local schema markup is present (Organization and LocalBusiness types).
- **What good looks like:** Schema validated correctly with Google’s Structured Data Testing Tool.
- **How to fix it:** Implement JSON-LD structured data on homepage covering business name, address, phone, and services.

### 7. Image Optimization
- **What to check:** Review images for alt tags and file size.
- **What good looks like:** All images have descriptive alt text that includes relevant keywords; file sizes are optimized to load quickly.
- **How to fix it:** Add relevant alt text to all images and compress files for faster loading times.

## MEDIUM PRIORITY (Fix This Month)

### 8. Content Updates
- **What to check:** Review existing content for topical authority and relevance.
- **What good looks like:** Blog posts and services pages contain 800+ words and include local keywords, answering FAQs related to barber services.
- **How to fix it:** Expand content on service pages, focusing on unique selling points, local trends, and tips for residents in Antioch.

### 9. Internal Linking Structure
- **What to check:** Assess internal linking between service pages and blog content.
- **What good looks like:** Logical links that lead from service pages to related blog posts or service details, improving navigation.
- **How to fix it:** Add hyperlinks within content that guide users to relevant pages.

### 10. Review & Increase Backlink Profile
- **What to check:** Analyze existing backlinks using tools like Ahrefs or Moz.
- **What good looks like:** Quality backlinks from local businesses, blogs, or media.
- **How to fix it:** Reach out to local blogs or news sites for features or reviews. Consider local sponsorships or partnerships to increase backlinks.

## QUICK WINS (Takes < 30 min Each)

### 11. Optimize Contact Page
- **What to check:** Review contact page for completeness.
- **What good looks like:** Correct business name, address, phone number, hours of operation, and a clear contact form.
- **How to fix it:** Ensure all information is accurate and up-to-date.

### 12. Google Analytics & Search Console Setup
- **What to check:** Ensure these tools are linked and data is tracking correctly.
- **What good looks like:** Both tools show traffic, keywords, and search performance.
- **How to fix it:** Link or reconfigure accounts to track website performance effectively.

### 13. Check NAP Consistency
- **What to check:** Review name, address, phone number (NAP) on the website for uniformity.
- **What good looks like:** Exact same NAP used across the website and Google Business Profile.
- **How to fix it:** Update any discrepancies found on the website or GBP.
