## Tips to Maintain Your Child's Fade at Home

### 1. Regular Brushing and Combing

A fade requires regular maintenance to keep it looking fresh. Parents should encourage their children to brush their hair daily and use a comb to maintain the structure of the fade. This simple step can prevent tangles and keep the haircut sharp.

### 2. Appropriate Hair Products

Using the right hair products can significantly prolong the life of your child’s fade. We recommend lightweight styling creams or pomades specifically designed for kids. Avoid heavy gels that can lead to buildup, as this can weigh the hair down and impact the fade's appearance.

### 3. Scheduling Regular Haircuts

To maintain that fresh fade, regular haircuts are essential. Generally, kids should return for a haircut every 4 to 6 weeks, depending on how quickly their hair grows. ## Incorporating Routine Care Practices for Lasting Style

### 1. Washing Hair

Teach your child the importance of keeping their hair clean. Regular washing with a mild shampoo can help maintain both hygiene and style. However, it’s important to wait at least 48 hours after getting a haircut before washing, to allow the fade to set properly.

### 2. The Role of Hair Clippings

During visits to our barbershop in Antioch, ask your child's barber for the leftover hair clippings. These can be an excellent way for parents to spot check their children’s hair growth and determine when the next haircut may be needed.
