## Post 2 — Fade vs Taper (Kids)

**Blog:** `/blog/fade-vs-taper-haircut-antioch`  
**Type:** Educational / Family

Parents — fade or taper for your kid?

**Fade:** shorter sides that blend up into longer hair on top. Clean, modern, stays sharp with regular touch-ups.

**Taper:** gradual length change, softer transition. Great for school photos and easier grow-out between visits.

Not sure? Bring a reference pic. Our barbers walk you through it before the first snip.
