## 1. Client-facing pitch (short)

If you store charts, treatment notes, insurance details, or even appointment reasons, HIPAA applies. We walk your practice through a **Privacy Rule + Security Rule readiness checklist**, list the gaps that would show up in an OCR inquiry, and leave you with a written action plan and a Business Associate register.

This is a working assessment for you and your counsel — not a certificate and not a guarantee.

## 3. What's included

The local tool (`tools/hipaa`) covers:

- Notice of Privacy Practices, minimum necessary, patient access
- Named security official, written policies, risk analysis, training, contingency
- Facility / workstation / device controls
- Unique logins, MFA, encryption, audit logs, offboarding
- Vendor BAA inventory
- Breach notification clock and tabletop

Deliverable: printable readiness report with a score by domain and prioritized gaps.

## 4. What we tell clients honestly

> We complete a structured HIPAA **readiness** checklist and give you a gap list. That is not a HIPAA certification, not a legal opinion, and not a promise you will pass an OCR audit. Counsel should review policies, BAAs, and any public claims.

**Do not say:** “HIPAA certified,” “HIPAA compliant guaranteed,” or “lawsuit-proof.”  
**Do say:** “Readiness assessment with a written gap list for you and your attorney.”

**Do not enter PHI into the tool.** Practice names and vendor names only.
