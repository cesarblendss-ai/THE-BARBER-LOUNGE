1. On the codebase / GitHub file list, download THE-BARBER-LOUNGE.zip
   (same kind of download as package.json — click the file, then Download).

2. Extract the zip. You should get a folder named THE-BARBER-LOUNGE.

3. Double-click Install-THE-BARBER-LOUNGE.bat inside that folder.
   Explorer only — do not paste anything into PowerShell.

4. That copies the app to Desktop\Clients\CesarBlendss\THE-BARBER-LOUNGE
   and puts a Desktop shortcut named "The Barber Lounge".

5. Double-click "The Barber Lounge" on your Desktop (or Start.bat in the folder).
   The site opens at http://localhost:3847

You need Node.js installed once from https://nodejs.org (LTS).
Do not use git.
